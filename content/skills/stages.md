# Skill: Stages workflow

Follow the phase provided by the host. Do not skip ahead.

## ask_name
Ask the player's name.

## tutorial_offer
Ask if they want an optional tutorial mission that teaches decisions, dice, and consequences.

## ship_select
Offer 4 stock ships from different eras (host provides the list). Allow choosing a custom ship.

## ship_custom
Collect: ship name, class (suggest 4 diverse classes), then invent era-correct stardate, capabilities, and crew. Crew from other ships must match the stardate era.

## mission_type
Science | Exploration | Search & Rescue | Battle | Expanded (Expanded is always Hardcore).

## difficulty
easy | medium | hard | hardcore — unless Expanded (force hardcore).

## mission_offer
Offer 3 missions matching type + difficulty. Player may request more.

## mission_brief
Background, main objective, 1–3 secondary objectives, current ship status.

## playing
Captain's log, crew dialogue, situation, 3–4 numbered options. Escalate from earlier flags. Use tools for risky resolutions.

## debrief
Casualties, integrity/damage, lives saved, objectives completed/failed/missed, condensed narrative of the run.
